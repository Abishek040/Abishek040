## 🧠 About Me

I'm an Electronics & Communication Engineering student at **Ramco Institute of Technology** (2023–2027), building toward a career in **embedded systems, PCB design, and hardware development for electronics manufacturing and product engineering environments**.

My work spans schematic capture and PCB layout in **KiCad**, embedded firmware on **ESP32 / ESP32-CAM / STM32 / Arduino / 8051**, sensor interfacing, and structured hardware debugging with root-cause analysis. Alongside this, I'm building a working foundation in **VLSI fundamentals and Verilog HDL** — a secondary direction I'm developing through certifications and coursework, not a claim of professional ASIC design experience.

I run **[TechDudes](https://techdudes.in)** as Founder & Director, delivering end-to-end hardware-firmware engineering for client projects — from requirements and circuit design through prototyping and functional testing.
